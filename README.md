An Aatrox bg3 mod for a true raid boss character.

E - Dash
W - Pull
Q1/2/3 - Devastaging Area damage
R - Huge power-up! Now with sounds!

The passive gives you huge healing.

All your powers scale naturally with your level.

Thank you JIN for the work on the initial mod before official mod support from Larian.
